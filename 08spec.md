Version 0.8 Achievements

Remove the x10 and x100 multiplier.  It wasnt balanced

Instead of only 3 quantity per item in the market, User can buy items as long as it is less than 50% of their current reel.

Add a 4th Tab: Achievements
Displayed is a list of achievements the user can obtain.
Each achievement has a title and unlock condition

"How do you like them Apples" : Buy one apple icon from the Market
"Second Breakfast" : Gain at least 2 Apples in one SPIN
"Out of Stock" : 50% of the reel is the same icon
"SSS" : Have 3 silver in the reel
"I understand it now": Prestige with an icon worth 1 silver or more
"Coin Collector": Have one of each coin (copper, silver, gold) in the reel
"Be Water, My Friend": Swap cells resulting in SPIN rewards from two icons
"WHY!!!": Block a column resulting in less SPIN rewards
"Born with a Diamond Spoon" : Prestige with Crown
"This is Sparta": Reach 300 Crowns in currency (Replaces the former win condition of 100 crowns)
"Ancient Civilization" : Reach 5000 Crowns in currency 
"WIP1": Will be unocked in the future
"WIP2": Will be unocked in the future
"Master of Elements" : Gain at least 1 of each element (air, water, earth fire) in one SPIN (replaces the former master of elements effect)
"Hapilly Ever After": Unlock the other 14 achievements


When a player performs an action that results in unlocking an achievement, display the achievement dialog.  The achievement should also be highlighted positively in the Achievement list.  Once unlocked, the achievement will no longer lock backwards.
